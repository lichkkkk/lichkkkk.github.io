I intend to use this blog post to summarize my learnings of LLM self-improvements, from various open blog posts, papers and my own tiny experiments.

## Blog Posts I Read

### [First Steps Toward Automated AI Research](https://www.recursive.com/articles/first-steps-toward-automated-ai-research) by Recursive

They shared SOTA results (obtained by their auto-research agents) on 3 benchmarks. Two of them are related Karpathy's work (AutoResearch and GPT-2), while the third one is a Nvida benchmark.

They shared their final solutions and some analysis on the trajectories (e.g. how their solution auto optimized NanoGPT to the sota loss).

The next step for me is to play with one benchmark by myself (e.g. auto-research) and then I could get a better idea about the loop and the value of their improvements.
